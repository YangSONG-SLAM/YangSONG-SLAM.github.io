I received the B.S. and M.S. degrees from the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Beijing Institute of Technology, in 2017 and 2020, respectively, and the Ph.D. degree from the Robotics Institute, University of Technology Sydney, in 2025. I am currently a Research Associate at The University of Manchester, UK. My research interests include extended Kalman filtering, SLAM, and optimization.
